# Brand rules override the design spec for the mark and the light

The approved landing design rotates the mark on the Principles cards, aims the hero light at the touch point, separates the square and the circle in the Approach panel, and uses white in places. The brand kit forbids rotating, recolouring, separating or adding effects to the mark, and the palette is ink, paper and vermilion. Where they disagree, the brand kit wins, with these readings:

- Principles cards use the supplied on-dark mark, unrotated, on all three cards.
- In the hero the mark sits above the light, unfiltered; the light splashes behind it, never on it.
- In the Approach panel the square and circle are abstract geometry while apart, and become the supplied mark file only when they touch. This keeps the page's central story (two disciplines meeting at one point) without altering the logo itself.
- White is allowed only inside supplied brand files and as the hot core of the light (shader core, Approach flare centre). Everything else uses paper.
