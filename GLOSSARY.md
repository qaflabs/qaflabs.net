# Qaf Labs site

The public website of Qaf Labs, a software product lab. These terms name the brand pieces and page parts that the design, the brand kit and the code all refer to.

## Brand

**Mark**:
The symbol alone: an ink (or white, on dark) square and a vermilion circle that touch at one point.
_Avoid_: Icon, logo (when you mean the symbol only)

**Logo**:
The mark together with the Qaf Labs name, as supplied in the brand kit.
_Avoid_: Wordmark (when you mean mark plus name), lockup

**Wordmark**:
The "Qaf Labs" name set in Geist SemiBold, which exists only as part of the logo.

**Touch point**:
The single point where the square and the circle meet; the sharp corner of the square.

**Light**:
The vermilion beam and its glow, the page's one light source on the dark canvas. Light may sit behind the mark, never on it.
_Avoid_: Laser (outside the code), glow (as a name for the whole effect)

**Brand colors**:
Ink, paper and vermilion, plus their alphas. White appears only inside supplied brand files and as the hot core of the light.

## Page

**Hero frame**:
The illustration below the headline showing design, code and phone preview panes; the surface the light lands on.
_Avoid_: Screenshot, mockup (for this element)

**Capability**:
A kind of software Qaf Labs builds (web apps, mobile, desktop, AI products, e-commerce, design systems).
_Avoid_: Service, offering

**Capability band**:
The two rows of capability words that move with scroll, below the hero.
_Avoid_: Marquee, ticker

**Manifesto**:
The one light (paper) section, stating what Qaf Labs believes.

**Approach step**:
One of the five stages of how Qaf Labs works: Discover, Design, Engineer, Ship, Evolve.
_Avoid_: Phase, stage (on the page)

**Principle**:
One of three short statements of how Qaf Labs holds the work.
_Avoid_: Value

**Illustration**:
Any mockup on the page that shows the kind of work, never a real client product.
_Avoid_: Screenshot, case study
